Booking types

    Workload-aware availability

    A 0–100 score sets how many slots a day can offer: light 4, normal 2, busy 1, heavy 0. In-person days stay closed. Load mode can cap that further: normal uses the score only; heavy allows at most 2 a day and 6 a week; ultra-heavy allows 1 a day and 3 a week. Off-calendar load (writing, builds) is added to booked time. Project titles stay on this machine. Add [focus], [meeting], or [deadline] to an event title to override its project default.
